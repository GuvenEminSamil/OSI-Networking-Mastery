What is the difference between error detection and error correction?	Detection identifies possible corruption; correction attempts to repair it. (02-04)
What is a parity bit?	An extra bit that makes the total number of 1s follow an even or odd rule. (02-04)
What does CRC stand for?	Cyclic Redundancy Check. (02-04)
Which binary operation is used in modulo-2 addition?	XOR. (02-04)
What is the Ethernet FCS?	A four-byte field carrying the CRC-32 value used to detect frame corruption. (02-04)
What does a switch normally do with a frame that has an invalid FCS?	It discards the frame. (02-04)
Does Ethernet retransmit a frame after an FCS failure?	No. A higher-layer protocol such as TCP may recover. (02-04)
