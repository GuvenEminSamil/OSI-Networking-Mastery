What carries data through optical fiber?	Light signals. (01-07)
What is the role of the core?	It guides light through the fiber. (01-07)
What is total internal reflection?	Light reflects back into the higher-index core instead of passing into the lower-index cladding. (01-07)
What is the main difference between SMF and MMF?	SMF has a smaller core and is commonly used for longer links; MMF has a larger core and is common for shorter links. (01-07)
Which wavelength is commonly used with 1000BASE-SX?	850 nm. (01-07)
What is modal dispersion?	Different light paths in MMF arrive at different times and spread the pulse. (01-07)
What are LC and SC?	Common fiber-optic connector types. (01-07)
What are SFP and SFP+?	Pluggable transceiver form factors; SFP is commonly Gigabit and SFP+ commonly 10 Gigabit. (01-07)
