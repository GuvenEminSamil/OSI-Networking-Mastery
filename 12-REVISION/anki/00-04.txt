What is voltage?	The electrical potential difference between two points, measured in volts (V). (00-04)
What is current?	The rate at which electric charge flows, measured in amperes (A). (00-04)
What is resistance?	How strongly a material opposes current flow, measured in ohms (Ω). (00-04)
What is Ohm's law?	V = I × R. Voltage equals current multiplied by resistance. (00-04)
How do you calculate electrical power?	P = V × I, measured in watts (W). (00-04)
Why can higher voltage reduce current for the same power?	Because I = P / V. Lower current can reduce cable power loss, which grows with I² × R. (00-04)
What is differential signaling?	A method that represents a signal using the voltage difference between two wires. Similar noise on both wires can cancel. (00-04)
What does PoE provide over an Ethernet cable?	Both network data and electrical power for compatible devices. (00-04)
